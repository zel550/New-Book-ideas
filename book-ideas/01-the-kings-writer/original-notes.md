# The King's Writer — Original Notes

_Source: "The king's writer.docx" (Google Drive, last modified 2023-12-14)_

A writer in medieval times (1500's?) writes erotic novels, becomes very popular, but is ultimately unfulfilled. The king takes notice and offers him or requests him to go in the palace and write his novels just for the king. The King starts demanding more and more extreme novels and depictions of sexual acts. Eventually, the writer comes up with his most extreme act- a novel, where at the end, the main character kills the woman he's sleeping with after sex by punching her to death. The king is exalted and wants the writer to reenact the scenes from his novels. The Writer thinks the King's requests a play, but the King says "no", he wants the writer to actually perform the acts in his novels. Knowing that would mean killing a person, the writer becomes despaired. He searches for a prostitute on the streets and chooses one, knowing he will take her life.

He has sex with her and when the times come, strangles her and hits her until she's dead in front of the king, who, hidden behind some curtains, is watching with excitement.

The King requests these acts more often in the following days.
