# Son Looking for His Father, the God of Music — Original Notes

_Source: "Book idea - son, looking for his father, the God of Music.docx" (Google Drive, last modified 2020-08-20)_

A bad-ass warrior with a chip on his shoulder is looking for his father. Scene where he finds him in a bar and it turns out his father is the God of Music (or Rock), banished from the Heavens, his heavenly guitar locked up in a room, for him never to touch it (as he caused some great tragedy when his music went out of control). The son is disappointed of who his father is, as he thought his father was also a bad-ass warrior (Think son is Jason Momoa, father is Jack Black). While son fights his nemesis and his army and is in trouble, his father goes back to the heavens, and manages to free his guitar from its chains, playing once again the Heavenly Rock, defeating the Gods and coming down to aid his son, ultimately defeating the villain.
