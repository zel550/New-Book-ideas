# The Guru Who Drank, Stole and Cursed — Original Notes

## Full original text

A book about a boy born as the next guru, the next spiritual leader of a nation, everybody regards him as a holy man and he is told to act like one, but he doesn't want to and feels trapped. However, he can't escape from this role as the whole world sees him as a spiritual leader and whatever he does is holy. He drinks, steals and curses and yet everybody still regards him as a messiah.
