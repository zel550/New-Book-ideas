## Context: Book 1, for reference

This note is explicitly pitched as a possible second book to an already-drafted novel found in the same Drive account under several working titles: **"The King and the Red Evil"** / **"The King, the Ruby, and the Red Evil"** / **"The King who fell from the sky: Alternative — The King who forgot."** That manuscript (multiple drafts and an ebook-proof version exist, ~50+ pages sampled) is a finished or near-finished project, not a brainstorm note, so it is not reproduced here — only summarized for context, since Book 2 depends on it:

- **Frame story (real world):** Cayden is a patient at Mercy Psychiatric Hospital, repeating "Guilty… Guilty…" in a dissociative rocking ritual. He has lost his family (implied, cause not confirmed in the sampled pages). His roommate Jean believes he is turning into a frog ("the Frog"); fellow patients Charles ("the Rabbit") and Lenny ("the Sloth") share similar animal-transformation delusions (clinical lycanthropy is named explicitly as their shared diagnosis). Cayden's psychiatrist is Dr. Dimitriou, nicknamed "Miss Perfect" / "Perfect" by the patients for her impeccable, unreadable composure — most patients distrust her.
- **Fantasy layer:** Interleaved chapters follow a wolf bounty hunter, Monsieur Géroux, in a besieged fantasy city (place names sampled: Agapea, Windhaven, the river Toska) with his wife Anna and two cub children, fighting fires from an attack while gripped by a surge of rage tied to something called "the Red Evil." (Note: this wolf-bounty-hunter character and his fighting philosophy strongly echo the separate, unrelated brainstorm document for "Мосю Живаков" — worth flagging to the author as either an intentional shared-universe/character reuse or two independent drafts of the same figure; see that project's outline.)
- **Implied Book 1 structure (from the Book 2 note's references):** Cayden's psyche has fractured into at least three parts — "Little Cayden" (child self), "Real Cayden" (his authentic self), and "Evil Cayden" / "The Protector" (a defensive alter who also carries "The Red Evil," the story's central trauma-antagonist). By the end of Book 1, "Real Cayden" is imprisoned inside a pleasant illusion by "Evil Cayden," who won't relinquish control.
