## Full original text

Presents two people that become a couple. A look on couples, that is more realistic than a Hollywood one, think 500 days of summer or Love. Read couple therapists opinions, youtube videos, etc. for typical problems, that couple face and how do they fix them.

Title: "The things we couldn't say, if we could" (of if we would, or without the second part)

A story of a couple, they meet, the ecstasy of finding your "soulmate", the first months of the relationship (happy, perfect), they move in into an apartment together, then little things start annoying them (forgetting to close doors, chewing with open mouth, forget to buy toilet paper, unwashed dishes, socks on the floor, etc.). The atmosphere becomes frosty, their relationship – toxic, the lively conversations from the first months are replaced by chilling silence and passive aggressive moves. It comes as it should – one of them moves out (if it's a straight relationship – the girl). As she is about to walk out the door, they share a look. She says something, maybe asks him something. A conversations starts about what they truly feel and about their true fears – fear of losing the other person, pride, etc. They reveal everything they have been afraid of and the reasons why they pushed the other person away (intense conversation, lots of yelling, crying, etc.). At the end they say "I love you. That's why we can't be together" or something to this effect, they can't be together in that moment, they have to get rid of their emotional baggage first.

It is revealed that conversation never happened, the woman is still standing at the door, looking at the guy, he's looking back, she leaves and shuts the door.

Think about visual style – colours, framing (when all is good, they are shown together in the frame, when everything starts to fall apart, they are show only separately in separate frames to underline them splitting apart), also MOVEMENT – when they are standing still, something has to be moving (a candle, a window might be open and the wind coming through, moving stuff), when they are moving, stuff can and probably should be standing still, to create contrast. Maybe bright colours in the beginning and faded colours in the end.

Movement: Left to Right - natural, good

Right to left – unnatural, bad

Characters standing on the right – good

Characters standing on the left – bad

A company uses quantum entanglement and quantum computers to come up with a new product – the quantum transporter 1.0. For the first time in human history, people have cracked how to transport matter using this new technology. A CEO unveils the machine at a tech conference under the cheering of the audience. Everyone is in love with this new, amazing piece of technology. But what will be the consequences to society and to the personal lives of our protagonists? What dark secrets about this newly unveiled piece of technology does the CEO and his engineers hide?

An engineer, working for the company, unveils the horrific truth, hidden behind the technology…
