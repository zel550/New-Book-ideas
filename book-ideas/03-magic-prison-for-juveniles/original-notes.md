# Magic Prison for Juveniles — Original Notes

_Source: "Magic Prison for Juveniles (like Deadly Education, but in prison).docx" (Google Drive, last modified 2022-05-18)_

The source document contains no body text — only the title, which doubles as the pitch:

> Magic Prison for Juveniles (like Deadly Education, but in prison)

The comp title, Naomi Novik's *A Deadly Education* (the Scholomance trilogy), centers on a magical school that is itself an active hazard — students must survive the building/institution as much as any villain. The outline below extends the one-line pitch into a full concept: a magical juvenile-detention facility that is, structurally and magically, trying to kill its inmates, and a protagonist who has to figure out how to survive both the institution and the reason she was sent there.
